Tabby payment FAQs

Can I pay for a family visa or visa renewal with Tabby?

Yes. Tabby is available for QuickDocs UAE family visa application and renewal services. Request your quote and tell us you would like to use Tabby. Each payment is subject to Tabby approval.

Which other QuickDocs services accept Tabby?

Tabby is available across all our services, including MOFA and full attestation, legal translation, PCC, Emirates ID, certificate equivalency, newborn residency support, medical fitness and OHC typing, and lost passport certificate support.

Can I pay in instalments?

Tabby can offer instalment options for eligible purchases. Check the plans offered for your payment, including the first payment, later due dates and any charges, before confirming. QuickDocs does not decide which plan Tabby offers you.

Does the Tabby payment include government fees?

Ask QuickDocs for an itemised quote showing service charges and any applicable government or third-party fees. Confirm the exact amount to be paid through Tabby before proceeding.

Is approval guaranteed?

No. Tabby assesses the payment and determines the available plan. A successful Tabby payment also does not guarantee a visa, certificate or other authority approval.

Who can help with my service or payment?

Contact QuickDocs about your quote, documents, service progress or a cancellation request. Use Tabby support for account access, repayment dates, payment-plan questions or a declined payment. Review both your service terms and the Tabby terms shown before paying.

Need a quote for your service?

Send the service name and mention Tabby. We’ll explain the next steps and the scope of your quote before you proceed.
