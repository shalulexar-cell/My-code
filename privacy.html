Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Information you send us

When you contact QuickDocs UAE, you may choose to provide contact details and information or documents relevant to the service you are asking about.

How we use information

We use information you provide to respond to enquiries, explain requirements, provide requested document or typing support, communicate about your request and maintain necessary business records.

WhatsApp and external services

Our website can open WhatsApp and may link to third-party or government websites. Information you send through those services is also subject to their own privacy practices.

Document care

Please send only documents needed for the service. We aim to handle customer information responsibly and limit its use to the requested service and related administration.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact info@quickdocsuae.com.

Optional website analytics only operate when a measurement ID is configured and you grant analytics consent. If enabled, analytics measures page visits and clicks on service, WhatsApp, phone and email buttons, but the website must not send passport numbers, certificate contents, names, email addresses, phone numbers or prefilled WhatsApp message text to analytics. A WhatsApp click does not confirm that a message was sent. You can withdraw permission by clearing this site's saved data in your browser, or contact info@quickdocsuae.com for privacy questions.
