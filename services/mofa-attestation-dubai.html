MOFA attestation vs full attestation

UAE MOFA attestation is the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs step. Full attestation normally means the required steps in the country that issued the document first, followed by the UAE-side requirements. Which one you need depends on the document and where it was issued. See our MOFA attestation guide for details.

Documents commonly attested

  • ✓Marriage and birth certificates
  • ✓Degree certificates and academic transcripts
  • ✓Commercial and company documents
  • ✓Powers of attorney and other legal documents

Which document attestation route applies?

UAE MOFA distinguishes UAE-issued and foreign-issued documents. For documents issued in the UAE, MOFA chooses digital attestation or an approved courier route based on the issuing authority and document type. The correct procedure depends on whether the original document is digitally verifiable.

UAE-issued documents

Check the issuing authority, intended destination and eligible original digital document. If an eligible digital attestation route is unavailable, MOFA may direct you to an approved courier route.

Foreign-issued documents

First verify which authentication steps the issuing country and UAE mission require. Country-specific mission availability and the receiving authority's requirements can vary.

Read the official MOFA document attestation service for current routes.

Checklist before submission

  • Original paper document, or eligible original digital document.
  • Prior authentication by competent authorities where necessary.
  • English or Arabic, or a legally certified translation when required.
  • Unlaminated paper originals for courier attestation.
  • Document's country of issue and intended use.

Certificates and transcripts may count as separate documents. Fees depend on the official service selected; QuickDocs support fees are quoted separately.

For Indian documents, verify the July 2026 outsourced-consular-service change before booking an appointment.
