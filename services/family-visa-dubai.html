Who can be sponsored

Residents commonly sponsor a spouse and children. Sponsoring parents or other relatives is treated differently and has stricter conditions. Eligibility depends on your visa category, income, family relationship and housing, and these rules can change, so we confirm the current conditions with you first.

Salary guidance

For salaried residents sponsoring a spouse or child, the monthly income requirement is AED 4,000, or AED 3,000 with employer-provided accommodation. A labour contract showing AED 3,000 alone does not establish eligibility: the accommodation arrangement and suitable family housing must also be verified. GDRFA determines eligibility for Dubai applications.

Salary guidance checked on 4 October 2026: GDRFA family entry permit · ICP income and employer-provided housing conditions · GDRFA parents / humanitarian residence. Requirements can change; confirm your case with GDRFA or Amer before applying.

Children and certificate language

A resident can normally sponsor sons up to age 25 and unmarried daughters without the same age limit. Certificates already issued in English or Arabic should not automatically be sent for translation; documents in other languages may need an applicable legal translation depending on the application.

Inside UAE vs outside UAE family visa

A new family visa application is not identical in every case. If the family member is outside the UAE, the entry-permit route is used before completing the residence steps. If the family member is already inside the UAE, the application route and applicable government charges can differ. Tell us where each dependent is currently located so we can prepare the correct application.

Employee, investor or partner sponsor

Family sponsorship is not limited to salaried employees. Investor and partner residence holders can also have family-sponsorship routes. Instead of an employee's salary/employment documents, partner or investor cases can require company documents such as the partnership agreement or Memorandum of Association (MOA), trade licence and partner-related company documents, depending on the case.

Parents sponsorship and tenancy

GDRFA's humanitarian residence service lists AED 10,000 monthly salary for employed sponsors of parents, supported by a salary certificate or employment contract, suitable attested tenancy and three months of bank statements. Investor/partner sponsors have a separate company-document checklist. This route provides a one-year renewable permit and is subject to committee review; meeting the salary requirement does not guarantee approval.

Birth certificates for children

For a new child family-visa case, prepare the child's birth certificate early. GDRFA's family entry-permit requirements list an attested birth certificate, so a foreign-issued certificate may need the applicable attestation route before the visa application can proceed.

Refundable security deposits

For new investor/partner-sponsored spouse, child or adult-child applications, the refundable deposit used in our quote calculator is AED 3,054 per dependent, capped at AED 15,000 for those dependents. For a new parent visa, the refundable deposit is AED 5,000 per parent whether the sponsor is salaried or investor/partner. Renewal applications do not carry this deposit.

Frequently asked questions

Can I sponsor my family if I am on a Dubai residence visa?

Many residents can, but it depends on visa category, income, family relationship and housing. Send us your details and we will confirm the current conditions.

Do I need to attest my marriage certificate?

A marriage certificate issued outside the UAE normally needs to be attested before it is accepted. We can advise on the route for your country of issue.

Can you help if my family is outside the UAE?

Yes. The steps differ depending on whether your family is inside or outside the country, and we explain the route that applies to you.
