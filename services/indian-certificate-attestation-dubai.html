Indian Certificate Attestation Dubai

Indian-issued documents may require verification and authentication in India, a UAE mission or relevant consular step, and UAE MOFA attestation. The exact sequence depends on the document, prior attestations and the receiving authority.

Get requirements & service fee →Check my document

QuickDocs is an independent documentation-support service, not a government authority. Final fees, approvals and requirements are determined by the relevant authorities.

Who is this service for?

Indian-issued degrees, marriage certificates, birth certificates and other eligible documents.

How the document process works

  1. 1

    Check the issuing authority

    Identify the state, university or civil authority that issued the original certificate. Request a clear scan for a preliminary review; originals may be necessary at official stages.

  2. 2

    Review Indian authentication

    The Government of India and relevant state authorities publish document authentication and attestation routes. These vary by certificate category.

  3. 3

    Verify the UAE mission route

    Confirm whether UAE mission legalisation or applicable Indian consular attestation is required for your exact use case.

  4. 4

    Complete UAE-side steps

    Check MOFA’s current requirements, translation needs and whether your document has all prerequisites.

Prepare before enquiring

  • Document type, country of issue and intended use.
  • What attestations, stamps or verifications have already been completed.
  • Whether the document is an original paper certificate or an eligible original digital document.
  • Any deadline set by the receiving authority.

Please do not send passport numbers or sensitive certificate scans through the public website checker. A QuickDocs adviser can advise on secure document submission if required.

Frequently asked questions

Is apostille always enough for a UAE application?

Do not assume that an apostille alone is sufficient. Confirm the specific legalisation requirements with the UAE receiving authority and the relevant mission.

Do you support certificates from any Indian state?

We can review your document and explain potential next steps. Actual handling and available routes are confirmed before accepting a service request.

Where do I book Indian consular appointments in 2026?

The Embassy announced a new outsourced service arrangement in July 2026. Always check its current official announcement and appointment arrangements before travelling.
