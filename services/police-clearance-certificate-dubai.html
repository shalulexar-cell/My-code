Frequently asked questions

Who needs a police clearance certificate?

Usually people applying to another country or employer that asks for proof of good conduct.

Is the PCC valid forever?

Certificates are generally accepted for a limited period by the requesting authority. Ask the receiving body how recent it must be.

Does the PCC need to be attested?

Often yes if it will be used abroad. We can explain the next steps once you know where it will be used.
