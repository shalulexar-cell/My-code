What is a Dubai Police Lost Certificate?

Dubai Police provides a Lost Certificate service for lost documents and official or unofficial certificates, as well as certain other lost items. Lost passport cases are one of the common reasons customers contact QuickDocs.

How QuickDocs helps

  • ✓ Check the type of lost-document request
  • ✓ Help prepare the required applicant details and supporting documents
  • ✓ Assist with the online application process
  • ✓ Help you understand the next step after the police certificate is issued
Important:

QuickDocs UAE is an independent document-support service. We do not issue Dubai Police certificates and we are not Dubai Police. Approval, requirements, fees and processing are controlled by the relevant authority.

Is Tabby available for lost passport certificate?

Yes. QuickDocs UAE offers Tabby for lost passport certificate support in Dubai. Ask for an itemised quote and mention Tabby before paying. Your available payment plan and approval are determined by Tabby. Read our Tabby payment guide for the steps, covered services and payment FAQs.
