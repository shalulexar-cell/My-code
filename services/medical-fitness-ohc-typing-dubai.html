What QuickDocs does

We help prepare and type the applicable online application. We do not conduct medical examinations, issue medical results or act as Dubai Health.

Medical Fitness application typing

Medical fitness screening forms part of the UAE residency process for applicable applicants. QuickDocs can check the information you provide and assist with the online application typing before you attend the relevant medical fitness facility.

OHC / Occupational Health Screening application

Some professions require Occupational Health Screening according to the job category or employer requirement. QuickDocs can assist with applicable OHC application typing and document preparation. The actual screening is completed at the relevant medical fitness facility.

Unified Health Screening

Dubai Health currently offers a unified route for eligible people who require both residency Medical Fitness and Occupational Health Screening. Whether this applies depends on the profession and current system route.

Documents commonly checked

  • ✓ Valid passport or Emirates ID, depending on the application
  • ✓ Residency / e-visa information where applicable
  • ✓ Recent photograph where required
  • ✓ Profession and employer-related information for OHC cases

Frequently asked questions

Does QuickDocs perform the medical examination?

No. We provide application typing and document support. Medical examinations are performed by the relevant approved facility.

Can you help with OHC application typing?

Yes. We can assist with applicable Occupational Health Screening application typing and document preparation.

Do all professions need OHC?

No. Occupational screening requirements depend on the profession, employer request and current official requirements.

Requirements and system routes can change. The relevant authority or medical fitness provider controls eligibility, examinations and results.
