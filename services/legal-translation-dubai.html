Why it matters

For legal and official use, UAE bodies commonly expect translations from an accredited legal translator rather than an informal translation. Using the wrong kind of translation can mean a rejected application and a repeat of the work.

How it works

Send a clear copy of the document and tell us which authority it is for. We confirm the translation requirement, coordinate the work and let you know if the document also needs attestation. You can learn about the attestation side in our MOFA attestation guide.

Is Tabby available for legal translation?

Yes. QuickDocs UAE offers Tabby for legal translation support in Dubai. Ask for an itemised quote and mention Tabby before paying. Your available payment plan and approval are determined by Tabby. Read our Tabby payment guide for the steps, covered services and payment FAQs.

Frequently asked questions

Do you translate into Arabic and English?

Yes, tell us the language pair you need and we will confirm.

Does a translation need attestation as well?

Sometimes. It depends on the document and the authority. We check this before starting.

Can I send a photo of the document to get started?

Yes, a clear photo or scan on WhatsApp is enough to confirm the requirement.
