Marriage Certificate Attestation Dubai

Marriage certificates may need official attestation for a new spouse residency application or another UAE process. Your country of issue, certificate language and existing stamps determine the likely route.

Who is this service for?

Couples preparing a new spouse sponsorship application or another process requiring a marriage certificate.

How the document process works

  1. 1

    Check the original certificate

    Confirm the issuing civil authority, names, dates and language before arranging legalisation.

  2. 2

    Review the country of issue

    For documents issued abroad, verify the required home-country authentication and UAE mission arrangements.

  3. 3

    Check UAE MOFA attestation

    Use the ministry’s current route and document eligibility rules, including translation where applicable.

  4. 4

    Connect the certificate to your application

    For new spouse sponsorship, prepare the applicable family visa checklist and other sponsor documents.

Prepare before enquiring

  • Document type, country of issue and intended use.
  • What attestations, stamps or verifications have already been completed.
  • Whether the document is an original paper certificate or an eligible original digital document.
  • Any deadline set by the receiving authority.

Please do not send passport numbers or sensitive certificate scans through the public website checker. A QuickDocs adviser can advise on secure document submission if required.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to attest a marriage certificate for a family visa renewal?

QuickDocs generally does not request the marriage certificate for a routine spouse family visa renewal; individual authorities may request additional documents in exceptional cases.

Do I have to translate my marriage certificate?

A legally certified translation may be required if the certificate is not in Arabic or English, depending on the receiving authority.

Does this page include the spouse visa government fees?

No. For family visa costs, use the separate family visa calculator. The attestation quote is based on the document and route.
