Newborn residency process in Dubai

For a baby born in the UAE, the practical order is: obtain the UAE birth certificate, arrange the baby's passport, apply for the newborn residence permit, then complete the Emirates ID application. QuickDocs UAE can help with document checks and online application typing for the residency and Emirates ID stages.

2. Passport

Documents commonly needed

  • ✓Baby's birth certificate and passport, as applicable

  • ✓Marriage certificate
  • ✓Sponsor's accommodation and salary documents, as applicable

Complete the newborn residence within four months

UAE immigration law requires the newborn's identity documents and residence to be arranged within four months from the date of birth. An administrative fine can apply after that period, so it is better to start the birth certificate and passport steps early.

Family sponsorship salary

For salaried residents sponsoring a spouse or child, the monthly income requirement is AED 4,000, or AED 3,000 with employer-provided accommodation. A labour contract showing AED 3,000 alone does not establish eligibility: the accommodation arrangement and suitable family housing must also be verified. GDRFA determines eligibility for Dubai applications.

Salary guidance checked on 4 October 2026: GDRFA family entry permit · ICP income and employer-provided housing conditions · GDRFA parents / humanitarian residence. Requirements can change; confirm your case with GDRFA or Amer before applying.
