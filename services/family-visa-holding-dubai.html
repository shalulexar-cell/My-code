Family Visa Holding Dubai

Family visa holding is different from a standard renewal or cancellation. QuickDocs UAE treats it as a separate service and checks the sponsor's situation before confirming the route.

Ask QuickDocs on WhatsApp

How we help

  • Review the sponsor's current and changing visa situation
  • Check whether the family-visa holding route is applicable to the case
  • Prepare the relevant document checklist
  • Support the online typing/application steps where applicable
  • Confirm the case-specific quote before proceeding

Documents to prepare

  • Sponsor passport, current residence visa and Emirates ID copies
  • Dependent passport and current residence visa / Emirates ID copies
  • Documents related to the sponsor's visa change or new visa process, where applicable
  • Any additional documents required for the specific holding request
Case-specific requirements: Final documents, government fees, eligibility and processing steps depend on the applicant's current visa status and the relevant authority. QuickDocs confirms the applicable checklist and quote before proceeding.

Frequently asked questions

Is family visa holding the same as cancellation?

No. Holding and cancellation are separate services and should be assessed separately.

Is every family visa eligible for holding?

Eligibility depends on the sponsor's and dependents' current status. QuickDocs checks the case before confirming the process.

Need a case-specific quote?

Send the sponsor and dependent's basic visa details on WhatsApp. We will confirm the current requirements before you proceed.
