Why renew early

Visas that run past their expiry date can attract fines and may complicate later applications. Starting the checks a few weeks before expiry gives you time to fix a missing document rather than pay for the delay.

What we usually need

  • ✓Current tenancy contract or proof of accommodation

Frequently asked questions

When should I start the renewal?

As early as you can, ideally a few weeks before expiry, so any document problem can be fixed in time.

Do I need a new medical test for renewal?

It depends on the visa type and the authority's current rules. We confirm this when we review your case.

What if the sponsor's job or salary changed?

Changes can affect eligibility, so tell us up front. We will review the documents and explain what applies.
