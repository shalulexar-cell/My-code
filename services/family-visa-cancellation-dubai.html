Family Visa Cancellation Dubai

Need to cancel a family residence visa in Dubai? QuickDocs UAE handles this as a separate service so the cancellation requirements and quote are clear from the start.

Ask QuickDocs on WhatsApp

How we help

  • Check the sponsor and dependent's current visa details
  • Confirm the applicable cancellation route before submission
  • Prepare the online typing/application support where applicable
  • Explain any documents that must be provided before cancellation
  • Provide a case-specific quote before proceeding

Documents to prepare

  • Sponsor passport, residence visa and Emirates ID copies
  • Dependent passport and current residence visa / Emirates ID copies
  • Any additional cancellation-related document requested for the specific case
Case-specific requirements: Final documents, government fees, eligibility and processing steps depend on the applicant's current visa status and the relevant authority. QuickDocs confirms the applicable checklist and quote before proceeding.

No. Cancellation is handled as a separate transaction with its own requirements and quote.

Can I get the price online?

Send the current visa details to QuickDocs and we will provide a case-specific quote rather than publishing an amount that may not apply to your case.
