Foreign university qualification recognition

Foreign university qualification recognition is handled by the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research (MoHESR). School-certificate cases use a different route. Read our updated recognition guide and consult the official MoHESR service for the current requirements.

Before you begin

Check the current ministry and verification-partner checklist before arranging attestation. Read about the attestation routes in our MOFA attestation guide or contact us with your certificate details.

Is Tabby available for certificate equivalency?

Yes. QuickDocs UAE offers Tabby for certificate equivalency support in Dubai. Ask for an itemised quote and mention Tabby before paying. Your available payment plan and approval are determined by Tabby. Read our Tabby payment guide for the steps, covered services and payment FAQs.

Degree attestation versus recognition

Attestation and academic qualification recognition are separate processes. See our degree certificate attestation guide for the document preparation steps.

Frequently asked questions

Who needs certificate equivalency?

Typically students and job seekers using a foreign certificate for study or work in the UAE.

Do my certificates need attestation first?

The required verification and attestation route depends on the qualification and the receiving authority. We review the current checklist before arranging additional work.
