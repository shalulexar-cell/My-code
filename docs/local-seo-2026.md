Working draft for owner review. Do not publish or activate customer messaging without approval.

## Google Business Profile

Keep the real-world name **QuickDocs UAE**, the verified service-area setup with the business address hidden, and only the areas genuinely served. Do not add search terms to the business name or list an unstaffed virtual office.

Suggested description (edit in the Google Business Profile dashboard after checking its current contents):

> QuickDocs UAE provides independent online document preparation and application typing support in Dubai. We help residents with new family visa and renewal applications, newborn residence visa documentation, Emirates ID typing, medical fitness and OHC typing, UAE MOFA attestation, legal translation, police clearance certificate and certificate recognition enquiries. Our team checks the document requirements for your case and explains the process before proceeding. Contact us on WhatsApp for a case-specific checklist and quote. We are not a government authority; approvals and official fees are determined by the relevant UAE authorities.

## Honest review collection

1. Obtain the actual **Ask for reviews** link from the verified Google Business Profile. Never invent a link or use a Google Maps place ID as a substitute without checking it opens the review form.
2. At intake, record separate customer consent for WhatsApp service updates and review requests, the customer phone number, and a yes/no opt-out flag in a secure client-management system. Avoid public spreadsheets of names, passports or case details.
3. Trigger after the QuickDocs service is completed for **all eligible consenting clients**, irrespective of outcome or perceived satisfaction. Approval by the immigration authority is not necessarily the same as completion of QuickDocs' service.
4. Use the neutral template below once per completed service, not repeated reminders, with a direct verified link. Do not offer benefits, request 5-star ratings, gate out dissatisfied customers or ask staff to coordinate a burst of reviews.
5. Log the sent time and message/template ID to prevent duplicates; respect opt-outs promptly.

### WhatsApp request copy

Hello {{1}}, thank you for choosing QuickDocs UAE. We hope our document support was helpful. If you have a moment, we'd appreciate your honest feedback about your experience: {{2}}. Thank you for supporting our small business.

Do not insert applicant nationality, application result, passport/ID number or other personal data in the message. Confirm WhatsApp's template category and approval in WhatsApp Manager. Outbound messages outside an active 24-hour customer-service window require an approved template on the WhatsApp Business Platform, and WhatsApp opt-in must be respected.

### What can be done with the current setup

- **Immediately:** staff can manually send the above text only to consenting customers when a service is completed (using the genuine link).
- **Fully automatic:** requires a protected backend/business-platform integration, verified sending phone, template approval, opt-in and a reliable completed-status event. A static public GitHub Pages website cannot securely hold API credentials or detect immigration approvals. Do not publish a client tracker or token in this repository.

## Measurement

Review monthly: GBP calls / website clicks / search terms, genuine new Google reviews, Search Console clicks and impressions to the two priority guides, clicks on their WhatsApp CTAs, and qualified enquiries. Avoid recording customer personal data in web analytics.
