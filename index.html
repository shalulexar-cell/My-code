FAMILY VISA DUBAI

Family visa support for Dubai residents

Need to sponsor a spouse or child in Dubai? Start with the correct documents and application route. QuickDocs UAE provides online typing and document support for new family visa applications and renewals.

Family sponsorship salary requirements

For salaried residents sponsoring a spouse or child, the monthly income requirement is AED 4,000, or AED 3,000 with employer-provided accommodation. A labour contract showing AED 3,000 alone does not establish eligibility: the accommodation arrangement and suitable family housing must also be verified. GDRFA determines eligibility for Dubai applications.

Parents follow a separate GDRFA humanitarian route with an AED 10,000 salary requirement for employed sponsors and case-by-case review.
