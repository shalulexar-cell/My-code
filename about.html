About QuickDocs UAE

QuickDocs UAE provides online visa, attestation and document support in Dubai and across the UAE. Contact us for a checklist and an itemised quote.

Your documents, handled with care

We help individuals, families and businesses organise documents and understand the next application step. Our support starts with your situation: a new family visa, a renewal, a certificate to attest or an application that needs accurate typing.

What we help with

  • Family visa applications and renewals
  • MOFA and full attestation support
  • Legal translation coordination
  • Emirates ID application support
  • Medical fitness and OHC typing
  • Police clearance certificate support
  • Certificate recognition document support

How to work with us

Tell us the service you need, the issuing emirate and whether the application is new or a renewal. We review the document requirements, explain the applicable steps and confirm a quote before you proceed. Government charges and QuickDocs service charges are explained separately.

Our service is online. Contact us to arrange the next step and confirm any document delivery requirements.
