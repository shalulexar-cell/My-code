Legal Translation in the UAE: What to Prepare

Understand when legal translation may be requested in the UAE, what documents are commonly translated, and how to prepare files before submission.

Quick answer

Legal translation is commonly needed when a document must be submitted in a language accepted by the receiving UAE authority. The exact translation and certification requirement depends on the authority and purpose.

When might a translation be needed?

Marriage and birth certificates, education documents, court-related papers, powers of attorney, contracts and other records may need translation depending on where they will be submitted. Do not assume every authority has identical requirements.

Arabic and English documents

Before ordering a translation, confirm the language and certification standard required by the receiving organization. This avoids paying for a translation that does not match the submission route.

Translation and attestation are different

Translation changes a document into the required language; attestation authenticates seals or signatures through the relevant authorities. Some cases require one, while others require both. See our MOFA attestation guide.

How to prepare your document

Send a clear, complete scan showing all pages, stamps and seals. Tell the provider exactly where the translated document will be submitted and what it will be used for. Names should be checked carefully against passports or other identification.

Why receiving-authority requirements matter

A translation that works for one purpose may not automatically satisfy another organization. QuickDocs can help you identify the document route, but the receiving authority makes the final acceptance decision.

Frequently asked questions

Is legal translation the same as normal translation?

No. Documents intended for official use may have specific translator or certification requirements.

Can I translate only part of a document?

That depends on the receiving authority. For official submissions, complete pages, stamps and relevant endorsements may need to be included.

Do translated documents also need attestation?

Sometimes. Translation and attestation are separate processes, and the required combination depends on the document and its intended use.

This guide provides general information. Requirements, fees and processing routes can change and may differ by case. Check the relevant UAE authority before submitting an application.
