Family Visa Renewal in Dubai

Renewing a dependent visa goes smoothly when your documents are ready. Use this guide to check what to prepare before you start.

Ask QuickDocs on WhatsApp
Quick answer

For a Dubai family visa renewal, start by checking the dependent's passport, current residence details and Emirates ID together with the sponsor's current documents. Do not assume the documents used for the previous application are still sufficient.

What can change since the last renewal?

The sponsor's employment, accommodation, passport, contact information or other case details may have changed. Government requirements can also change, so review the current route rather than copying the previous application.

Check expiry dates first

Look at the visa, Emirates ID and passport expiry for each dependent. A passport close to expiry can hold up the renewal, so renew it first if necessary.

Documents to prepare

  • ✓Current visa or Emirates ID of each dependent
  • ✓Valid passports
  • ✓Sponsor's passport, visa and Emirates ID
  • ✓Current tenancy contract or proof of accommodation
  • ✓Salary certificate or employment contract, as applicable

Common problems

  • ✓Expired tenancy contract
  • ✓Sponsor's salary or job changed since the last application
  • ✓Passport close to expiry
  • ✓Leaving the renewal too close to the expiry date
