Family Visa Dubai: A Practical Guide

A family visa lets a UAE resident sponsor eligible relatives to live with them. This guide explains the general process so you know what to expect.

Ask QuickDocs on WhatsApp
Quick answer

A Dubai family residence application depends on the sponsor, the family relationship, the applicant's location and the documents available. Check the current authority requirements before starting, especially for marriage or birth certificates issued outside the UAE.

New application or renewal?

A new family residence application and a renewal are different search intents and different workflows. If your dependent already has UAE residence, use our family visa renewal guide. If you are starting sponsorship for the first time, continue with this guide.

Who can sponsor

Residents can generally sponsor a spouse and children. Eligibility depends on the sponsor's residence category, income, family relationship and accommodation. For salaried residents sponsoring a spouse or child, the monthly income requirement is AED 4,000, or AED 3,000 with employer-provided accommodation. A labour contract showing AED 3,000 alone does not establish eligibility: the accommodation arrangement and suitable family housing must also be verified. GDRFA determines eligibility for Dubai applications.

Salary guidance checked on 4 October 2026: GDRFA family entry permit · ICP income and employer-provided housing conditions · GDRFA parents / humanitarian residence. Requirements can change; confirm your case with GDRFA or Amer before applying.

The main steps

  • ✓Confirm sponsor eligibility and gather the sponsor's documents
  • ✓Prepare and, where needed, attest and translate the family documents
  • ✓Submit the entry permit or status-change application
  • ✓Complete the medical fitness test when required
  • ✓Apply for the Emirates ID and have the visa stamped or issued

Common mistakes

  • ✓Applying with an unattested marriage or birth certificate
  • ✓Passports that are close to expiry
  • ✓Missing or out-of-date tenancy documents
  • ✓Starting the process without checking current eligibility rules

Inside-country and outside-country applications

Where the family member is located matters. A dependent outside the UAE normally follows the entry-permit route before completing residence formalities. An applicant already inside the UAE can have different application steps and additional inside-country charges. Always tell the typing provider the dependent's current location before requesting a quote.

Can an investor or partner sponsor family?

Investor and partner residence holders are also relevant sponsor categories. GDRFA documentation lists partnership documentation for partner cases, and investor/partner residence documentation includes a partnership agreement or MOA and trade licence. The exact family-sponsorship checklist depends on the sponsor's residence category and company structure.

Parents need a different checklist

GDRFA's humanitarian residence service lists AED 10,000 monthly salary for employed sponsors of parents, supported by a salary certificate or employment contract, suitable attested tenancy and three months of bank statements. Investor/partner sponsors have a separate company-document checklist. This route provides a one-year renewable permit and is subject to committee review; meeting the salary requirement does not guarantee approval.
