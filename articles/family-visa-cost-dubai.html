Official fee components in AED — not a complete package price
ServicePublished amount and conditions
Family entry permitAED 200 + 5% VAT (AED 210 on that base). For an applicant inside the country, the service card additionally lists AED 500 inside-country fee, AED 10 Knowledge Dirham and AED 10 Innovation Dirham. GDRFA entry-permit fees.
Residence permitAED 200 base, plus AED 10 Knowledge Dirham, AED 10 Innovation Dirham and AED 20 delivery. The service card also lists an AED 500 inside-country fee; confirm its application to your route. GDRFA residence fees.
Emirates ID — two yearsAED 300 in listed fees: AED 100 per residence year × 2, plus AED 100 smart-service fee. Urgent service and additional channel charges are not included. ICP new Emirates ID fees.
Standard medical fitnessAED 270 for the standard Dubai Health examination, listed at 24 working hours. Required for residence applicants aged 18 or above; it is not an under-18 child charge. Premium and domestic-worker packages have different prices. Dubai Health price list; Dubai government age guidance.
Sponsor file openingAED 230 in listed charges: AED 200 base + AED 10 VAT (5% of the base) + AED 10 Knowledge Dirham + AED 10 Innovation Dirham. For first-time sponsor registration when a new file is required, rather than a charge for each dependent. GDRFA sponsor-file fees.
Status amendment — if requiredAED 535 for the individual fee route: AED 500 + AED 10 Knowledge Dirham + AED 10 Innovation Dirham + AED 15 individual fee. The establishment fee is different. GDRFA status-amendment fees.
Existing residence cancellation — if requiredAED 120 in listed charges: AED 100 + AED 10 Knowledge Dirham + AED 10 Innovation Dirham. This is separate from a new residence application. GDRFA cancellation fees.

Before adding the amounts: GDRFA publishes inside-country charges on more than one service card, and status amendment is a separate service. The published cards do not establish which combination will be charged for every application. Confirm the applicable line items in the official application before calculating a complete total. Do not automatically add every row or every inside-country surcharge.

VAT is shown only where explicitly stated in the cited fee schedule; no blanket 5% has been added to all government fees. Insurance, certificate attestation, translation, deposits, fines, optional urgency, additional channel charges and QuickDocs service charges are not included in these figures. Fees can change; the authority's assessed amount at submission applies.

Quick answer

There is no responsible single price for every Dubai family visa case. The total can combine government charges, medical or Emirates ID steps when applicable, document attestation or translation, and a provider's service charge. Ask for an itemized quote for your exact case.

Why we do not publish a misleading fixed total

A headline price can look attractive while excluding steps that your case actually needs. QuickDocs prefers a case-specific breakdown so you can see what is a government or third-party charge and what is our service charge before proceeding.

What goes into the total

  • ✓Government fees for the application, which can change
  • ✓Medical fitness test for the family member, when required
  • ✓Emirates ID application fees
  • ✓Attestation and translation of certificates, if needed
  • ✓Service or typing charges from the company assisting you

What makes costs differ

  • ✓Whether the family member is inside or outside the UAE
  • ✓Number of family members being sponsored
  • ✓New application or renewal
  • ✓Whether documents need attestation or translation
  • ✓Urgent versus standard processing, where available

Inside-country applications can cost more

Government charges differ depending on whether the sponsored family member is already inside the UAE. Some sponsorship categories can also involve a refundable financial guarantee or security deposit. These are case-specific, so QuickDocs confirms the current amount before quoting rather than presenting one deposit as universal.

How to compare quotes

Ask what is included. A lower price may leave out government fees, medical tests or Emirates ID costs. Ask for a breakdown separating government fees from service charges.

Example: requesting a quote for a spouse and child

Send each person's age, whether they are inside or outside the UAE, whether this is a new application or renewal, and whether relationship certificates are already attested. Request a separate breakdown per person and identify any shared charges. This is a preparation example, not a price estimate.

New application versus renewal

Ask for the correct application type rather than comparing a renewal quote with a new-visa offer. Use our renewal preparation guide to check what has changed since the previous application.

Frequently asked questions

Why can't you give one fixed price?

Costs depend on your case, such as who you sponsor, where they are and which documents need work. We give a quote once we know.

Are government fees included in a service quote?

Ask each provider for a breakdown so you can see what is government fees and what is a service charge.

Do renewal costs differ from a new application?

They can. The steps and fees involved differ, so quote each separately.
