Medical Fitness & OHC Dubai: Documents and Steps

Compare Dubai residency medical fitness, occupational health screening and the unified route. Check documents and official Dubai Health application steps.

Which screening do you need?

Residency medical fitness is used for applicable residence applications and renewals. Occupational Health Screening concerns job-related health requirements. If your case requires both, ask whether Dubai Health’s unified route applies.

ScreeningPurpose
Medical fitnessMedical fitness certificate for an applicable residence transaction.
Occupational health / OHCHealth checks for the employer’s requested job category.
Unified Health ScreeningCombined screening for eligible applicants requiring both.

Residency medical fitness documents

Dubai Health lists a completed registration form, an original valid Emirates ID or passport, a recent white-background passport-size photo and an e-visa copy. Its journey starts with an application through Salem Portal, followed by attendance at a medical fitness centre and notification by SMS or email.

For dependent residence, the UAE Government states that sponsored family members aged 18 and above need to pass medical fitness tests at approved centres. Confirm the applicable transaction for each family member. If the screening relates to a dependent's residence renewal, see our family visa renewal support for the related document steps.

OHC documents and employer information

Dubai Health says some professions require Occupational Health Screening at the employer’s request, with checks based on job category. Its document list includes a registration form, original valid Emirates ID or passport, and a recent white-background photo.

Before typing, ask your employer to confirm the job category and required screening. Use the employer’s requested transaction details rather than assuming the residency medical application will satisfy the occupational requirement.

When the unified route applies

Dubai Health offers Unified Health Screening for eligible applicants who need both medical fitness and occupational screening, combining the required tests into one appointment. Check the participating centre and eligibility before registering.

Prepare for the appointment

  • Check the name and passport or ID details on the application.
  • Keep the registration reference with the original identity document.
  • Confirm the correct centre, screening category and appointment instructions.
  • Use a mobile number and email you can access for notifications.
  • Retain the official result and receipt for the related application.

If you have already registered, check the transaction before paying for another application. Ask Dubai Health about any amendment or result enquiry using your existing reference.

Fees and results

The chosen screening and service package determine the charge. Use the pricing link on the official Dubai Health medical fitness page for the current packages. QuickDocs confirms the typing/service fee separately.

Dubai Health’s medical fitness service page also links to result tracking. Use that official link after the exam and follow any further instructions from the centre.
