MOFA Attestation UAE: A Practical Guide

Attestation confirms that a document is genuine so UAE authorities will accept it. Here is how UAE MOFA attestation differs from full attestation.

Ask QuickDocs on WhatsApp
Quick answer

MOFA attestation is a UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs attestation step. For a document issued abroad, earlier authentication steps may be required before the UAE-side step. The correct route depends on the issuing country, document type and where the document will be used.

Important document checks before submission

The Ministry's current guidance says documents should be original, in English or Arabic (or accompanied by an official translation), and already attested by the appropriate authorities before submission where required. Laminated documents are not accepted for attestation. Always confirm the current requirements on the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs website before proceeding.

What is MOFA attestation?

MOFA is the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs. UAE MOFA attestation is the step where the ministry attests a document so it can be used officially in the UAE, or abroad if it is a UAE-issued document.

What is full attestation?

Full attestation normally means the required steps in the country that issued the document first, followed by the UAE-side requirements. The exact chain depends on the document and the country.

Documents that commonly need attestation

  • ✓Marriage and birth certificates
  • ✓Degree certificates and transcripts
  • ✓Company and commercial documents
  • ✓Powers of attorney

How to prepare

  • ✓Identify the document, the issuing country and where it will be used
  • ✓Check whether translation is needed
  • ✓Keep the original documents safe and clear copies handy
  • ✓Ask for the exact route before paying for any step

Frequently asked questions

Is UAE MOFA attestation the same as full attestation?

No. MOFA is the UAE ministry step; full attestation includes earlier steps in the issuing country.

Do UAE-issued documents need MOFA attestation?

Often when they will be used abroad. Check with the receiving body.
