UAE Certificate Recognition & Equivalency Guide

Which authority handles foreign university recognition?

The Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research (MoHESR) operates the service for recognition of university certificates issued outside the UAE. “Certificate equivalency” remains a common search term. Use the current ministry service, rather than an older Ministry of Education instruction page.

Ask the employer, university or other receiving organisation which qualification and report it needs. School certificates have a different route; a foreign university recognition checklist should not be used automatically for them.

Verification, recognition and attestation

  • Qualification verification checks the academic credential through the ministry’s approved route.
  • Recognition is the ministry’s official recognition process for the qualification.
  • MOFA attestation authenticates a document’s seals/signatures through its applicable attestation route.

An attested certificate does not automatically provide academic recognition. Identify the output the receiving organisation requires before purchasing additional document services.

Organise your academic documents

Start with the qualification certificate, identity documents and any academic records requested by the current service or verification partner. Keep the awarding institution, country, study dates and qualification title consistent across your application.

If you transferred between institutions, organise records from each institution and explain the transfer clearly. This preparation tip helps you respond to a request for supporting records; the actual required documents come from the ministry or approved partner.

Follow the current recognition route

MoHESR’s service guidance describes selecting an accredited verification partner and signing in with UAE Pass. Follow the current instructions for credential verification and the recognition request, retaining receipts and references for both stages.

Check any partner request against the ministry’s approved route. Use your own contact details so you receive amendment notices and the final outcome. Keep your UAE Pass login and approval under your own control.

Attestation and translation checks

Ask which original or translated documents are required for your qualification and destination. Confirm that requirement before commissioning work. The MOFA guide explains document attestation, while the ministry’s recognition service determines the academic application requirements.

If the application needs more information

Retain the returned request and compare it with the uploaded files. Reply with the specific missing or corrected evidence and keep the application reference. MoHESR has a reconsideration service for reviewing a recognition decision; follow its current conditions if that is the relevant next step.
