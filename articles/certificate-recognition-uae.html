UAE Certificate Recognition: What “Equivalency” Means Today

People still search for certificate equivalency in the UAE, but foreign higher-education qualifications use the Ministry of Education's University Certificates Recognition system.

Quick answer

For degrees issued by foreign higher-education institutions, the UAE Ministry of Education replaced the former University Qualification Equivalency system with University Certificates Recognition. “Certificate equivalency” remains a common search term, but recognition is the current official terminology for this process.

Equivalency vs recognition

The terminology matters when searching for the correct government service. The Ministry of Education announced the University Certificates Recognition system in 2023 to replace the previous University Qualification Equivalency system for degrees from foreign higher-education institutions.

Who may need recognition?

Recognition can be relevant when a foreign qualification is required for employment, further study or another official UAE process. Whether you need it depends on the organization requesting your qualification.

Prepare before applying

Check that your name and qualification details are consistent across your documents. Depending on the case, supporting academic records, authenticity verification, translation or attestation may be relevant. Always follow the current Ministry of Education service requirements.

Attestation is not the same as recognition

Document attestation and academic qualification recognition serve different purposes. An attested certificate is not automatically the same thing as a recognized foreign higher-education qualification.

Official source

For current rules, use the UAE Ministry of Education website and its University Certificates Recognition service. QuickDocs provides document-preparation support and does not replace the Ministry's decision.

Frequently asked questions

Is certificate equivalency still the official name?

For foreign higher-education degrees, the Ministry of Education uses the term University Certificates Recognition; many people still informally search for certificate equivalency.

Does attestation guarantee recognition?

No. Attestation and academic recognition are different processes.

Can QuickDocs issue the recognition?

No. Recognition decisions are made by the relevant UAE authority; QuickDocs can assist with document preparation and related support.
